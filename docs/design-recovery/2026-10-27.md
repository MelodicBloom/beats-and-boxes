## Repository-specific records

### D31 — Beats & Boxes: community infrastructure expressed through useful tools

**Use when:** When recovering the Philadelphia music initiative, VJ/music collaboration tools, event systems, shared equipment, or regenerative service design.

[Recovered intent] Beats & Boxes proposes live-music infrastructure, reciprocal gear/revenue flows, and creative community building. It connects producers, DJs, visual artists, organizers, and venues through practical production support rather than an event-brand landing page alone.

[Design DNA — proposed] A lively but operationally clear field kit: artist/showcase stories, equipment availability, run-of-show, rider templates, collaboration needs, and event receipts. Visual/audio work can demonstrate the creative layer while useful logistics remain readable.

[Observed boundary] The README's scene/geography and “active” language are source assertions, not evidence of partnerships, operating lending systems, revenue transfers, venue permissions, or completed events.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Treat real people/organizations as consenting participants, not seed-data endorsements. Separate proposed resource loops from actual obligations, reservations, payments, and event outcomes. Begin with one useful co-designed artifact and an actual steward.

[Reimagined horizon] A regenerative music/visual production commons with shared production knowledge, bounded services, and visible returns to collaborators.

[Carry-forward test] One real stakeholder can use a documented kit or coordination flow and explain what it improved. Do not count speculative community impact as achieved.
